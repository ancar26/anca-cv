## 5. May Timing — Is This Actually a Good Time?

### Spain: Not great (you're right)
- Spanish companies traditionally slow down significantly from mid-May through September
- Budget planning cycles don't align well with Q2 hiring
- Decision-making is slower due to Semana Santa aftermath and pre-summer mental mode
- Conclusion: Target Spanish companies *last* or skip them for now

### Rest of Europe: Actually one of the best windows
- **May–June is peak hiring season** across Netherlands, Germany, UK, Nordics, and Ireland
- Q2 budgets are live and companies want people onboarded *before* July–August slowdowns
- Recruiters are active and responsive (not on holiday yet)
- September is the other peak — but May-June is earlier = less competition
- **This is the right moment to hit.** Don't wait.

### Practical timeline suggestion
| When | What to do |
|---|---|
| Now (May) | Send all 80 InMails to EU/UK targets — Netherlands, Germany, Ireland, UK, Sweden first |
| June | Follow up on any who opened but didn't reply. Keep sending. |
| July–Aug | Slow down — most of Europe on holiday. Use time to improve profile, do a Playwright project on GitHub |
| September | Second wave — re-engage. September is the other big hiring peak. |
